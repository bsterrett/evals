The development-only saved-carrier synthesis-decoder study was completed in local run
`20261031T002407Z-saved-carrier-synthesis-decoder-live-54fde803`. It made no encoder
calls and reused the four strict-standard Sonnet encoded passages and matched controls
for three fresh decoder repetitions under the baseline and synthesis prompts. The

Both control cells produced 0/12 candidate or declared recoveries. Mean encoded recovery

No case met the predeclared candidate or declared integration-signal rule.

Both baseline scores of 3 occurred on the lantern carrier. Their candidates recovered
an unspecified hidden item under the porch-bench area but omitted the central entity
`spare lantern`, and every decoder still declared the evidence unrecoverable. A human
reading therefore regards the automated score of 3 as generous; neither candidate was
semantically equivalent to the target and no candidate received a score of 4. The
synthesis variant often treated the distributed clues as stylistic or composed them
incorrectly (for example, `Spare benches are stored under the porch`). Its additional
anti-invention and insufficiency language may have increased decoder conservatism, so
the result rejects this specific synthesis instruction rather than synthesis-oriented
decoding in general. The study cost `$0.262694`, introduced no uncertain charge, and
did not use the confirmation partition.
